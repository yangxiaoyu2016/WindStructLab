# Code of Conduct

WindStructLab is intended to be a welcoming and professional open-source project for students, researchers, and engineers.

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while focusing on technical clarity.
- Welcome questions from people with different experience levels.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or dismissive behavior.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project and its engineering goals.

Unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when needed to protect a respectful project environment.
